# Best-Looking Food Picture — Seared Salmon

A stunning overhead photograph of a luxurious gourmet dish: a perfectly
seared salmon fillet with crispy golden skin on a bed of vibrant green
herb risotto, garnished with edible flowers, microgreens, and a drizzle
of saffron sauce, on a dark slate plate. Warm golden lighting, steam
rising gently, rich colors — high-end restaurant presentation.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, 559 KB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Deterministic: the same bytes are written on every run.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program
- `entry.png` — the food picture (PNG, 1024×1024)
